FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Strong partnerships are built on transparency from day one

-

What makes a great fit for Yugen?

We work best with ambitious founders and purpose-driven teams who have real conviction, not just about their product, but about the change they’re trying to make in the world. Most of our clients are startups and scaleups in the climate, sustainability, or social impact space, at a critical inflection point. If you're serious about design as a lever for clarity, traction and growth (and you're not afraid of being challenged along the way) we’ll get along just fine.

-

What will the process look like?

Every project starts by getting clear on your vision, goals and what’s getting in the way. Once we’re aligned, we’ll shape a plan of action with key milestones, roles and a realistic timeline. Then we onboard the right collaborators, set up shared tools and get to work. From first kickoff to final handoff, you’ll know exactly where we are and what’s coming next.

-

How much does it cost to work with you?

Our signature engagements start at £20K, with pricing shaped around your stage and ambition. Whether you're building a brand from scratch, refining your pitch to investors, or redesigning a product, we’ll tailor scope to match impact.

-

Can you just build us a website?

Technically, yes. But only if it’s solving the right problem. We’re here to tackle real business challenges, not just design pretty pages. A lot of teams come to us for a website, only to realise the blockers run deeper: unclear messaging, weak positioning, or a brand that doesn’t reflect who they are. Our signature engagements dig into that upfront. If a website’s the right move, we’ll build one that actually works. But if there’s work to do first, we’ll help you get there, before wasting time (and money) on the wrong fix.

-

How big is the Yugen team?

We’re intentionally lean, a core team of 8, led by two founders, with deep experience across brand, product and storytelling. For bigger or more specialised projects, we assemble trusted collaborators we’ve worked with before. No middle layers, no handoffs. Just the right team, built around your brief.

-

Do you only work with startups?

Startups are a core focus, but not the whole story. We partner with mission-led brands at every stage, from pre-seed to Series A, IPO and beyond. What matters most is ambition and a willingness to evolve. Our work is about helping visionary teams sharpen their brand, scale with clarity and move with purpose wherever they are on the journey.
